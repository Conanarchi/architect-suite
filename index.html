The tools

Nine tools for the work nobody enjoys

Every tool opens in the same window and runs as one Undo step. AuditIt and MatchIt stay free after the trial.

TagIt

Tagging

Tag seven categories across as many views as you need, in one run.

  • Doors, windows, walls, floors, ceilings, furniture, lighting
  • Pick the tag family and type per category
  • Skips elements that are already tagged

JoinIt · UnjoinIt

Geometry

Join or unjoin walls, floors, columns and beams in bulk.

  • Active view, selection or entire model
  • Only elements that really intersect are joined

GrabIt

Coordination

Copy every column type from the structural link, ready for Copy/Monitor.

  • Preview: new types versus types already in the model
  • Copies only what is missing

MatchIt

Free

Copy one element's graphic overrides onto others, or clear them.

  • Pick a source and targets in the model
  • Works in the active view

StampIt

Revisions

Write approval, revision or phase values on all sheets at once.

  • Up to four parameters per run
  • {date}, {user} and {phase} fill in automatically

NumberIt

Numbering

Renumber doors, windows, parking or rooms in a logical order.

  • Left to right, bottom to top, or along a line you draw
  • Prefix, start, step, digits, with a live preview: D-01, D-02

AuditIt

Free

See what is slowing a model down, without changing anything.

  • Warnings, in-place families, imported and oversized CAD
  • Views not placed on sheets

FinishIt

Finishes

Create internal finish walls room by room.

  • Offset into the room, not room-bounding
  • Room height or a custom height in mm

ScheduleIt

Schedules

Create schedules for up to nine categories in one pass.

  • Sensible default fields per category
  • Never overwrites an existing schedule

Install

  3. Open Revit. The Architect Suite tab appears on the ribbon.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can I undo what a tool did?

Yes. Every run is a single Revit Undo step, named after the tool. Press Ctrl+Z once to revert the whole run.

What happens when the trial ends?

AuditIt and MatchIt keep working for free. The other seven tools show a short message with a link to buy Pro. Nothing in your models is affected.

Does it work on workshared models?

Yes, like any other edit. Make sure the elements are not borrowed by someone else, then sync as usual.

Is my model data sent anywhere?

No. The only thing sent is your Autodesk user ID, to Autodesk, to confirm your purchase. See the privacy policy.

Can I use it in my own language?

The interface is available in English, Arabic, French, Russian and Chinese. It works with any Revit interface language.

Do I need to pay again for each Revit version?

No. One licence covers every supported Revit version on your computer, including new releases while your subscription is active.

Privacy policy · last updated 4 October 2026

Privacy policy

Architect Suite is a Revit add-in published by the Architect Suite team. This policy explains what information it uses.

Information we use

To confirm that you purchased Architect Suite, the add-in sends your Autodesk user ID and our app ID to Autodesk's entitlement service. We do not receive your name, email address, project files or model data. Purchases are processed by Autodesk and PayPal under their own privacy policies.

Information stored on your computer

The add-in saves your language choice (settings.json) and your trial and licence status (license.json) in %AppData%\ArchitectSuite. These files stay on your computer and are not sent to us.

No tracking

Architect Suite does not collect analytics, usage statistics or model content, and does not share data with third parties beyond the entitlement check above.

Retention and removal

Uninstalling the add-in and deleting the %AppData%\ArchitectSuite folder removes all data it stored. Uninstalling also stops the entitlement check.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open a request at https://github.com/Conanarchi/architect-suite/issues.
